# Requirements Elicitation Notes

## Google Maps
- Shows real-time congestion and busy times
- Helps users estimate wait times at locations
- Limitation: does not consider user schedule or nutrition

## GMU Dining Website
- Provides menu and nutritional information
- Users must manually browse menus
- Limitation: no optimization or recommendations

## Nutrition Apps (MyFitnessPal)
- Tracks calories and macronutrients
- Helps users meet fitness goals
- Limitation: not integrated with dining locations

## Observations
- Users must switch between multiple apps
- No system combines time, nutrition, and budget
- Decision-making is manual and inefficient

## How This Influenced SmartDine
- We combine all inputs into one system
- Provide a single recommendation instead of multiple searches
- Focus on time efficiency and reducing decision fatigue
