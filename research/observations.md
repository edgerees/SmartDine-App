# User and Product Research Observations

## Current Student Problem
Students currently make dining decisions by manually checking maps, menus, and running into issues with overcrowdness/timing while balancing class schedules, nutrition goals, and budgets.

## Observations
- students have limited time between classes
- dining lines are unpredictable
- nutrition information is often separated from timing information
- budget and swipe constraints make harder to make a decision
- existing tools do not combine all relevant factors into one recommendation (there are calorie tracker apps but we want to address the students schedule, nutritian goals and budget to allow the student to get the most from their institutions dining halls.)

## Implication for SmartDine
SmartDine should unify time, nutrition, and budget constraints into one decision support recommendation.
