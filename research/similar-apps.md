# Similar Apps and Systems

## Categories Reviewed
- map/navigation tools
- dining menu websites
- food ordering apps
- campus dining tools

## Similar Apps
- Gooogle maps, ways
- doordash, grubhub, ubereats
- The underarmor calorie trackiing app, there are a number of calorie tracker apps but few actually give you recomendations on the meals to choose.
- In the GMU accounts site students can see how much they have on their cards in the Atrium app but thats another extra step.

## Key Lessons
- menus are often available, but not connected to timing and schedule needs
- map tools show traffic or congestion, but not the exact number of students in a location.
- current systems require students to combine multiple sources manually.
- SmartDine should reduce decision fatigue by combining these inputs in one place
